# Value Investing Checklist

Not hard cutoffs — each metric maps to a confidence band, not a pass/fail
gate (e.g. P/E < 20 → 0.7-0.9 confidence; 20-30 → 0.3-0.5; >30 → low/negative).

## General
- P/E < 20 (graded band, not a hard cutoff)
- Price/Sales < 1
- Operating margin > 10% (turning revenue into operating/net income)
- ROE > 12–15%
- PEG ratio (P/E ÷ growth rate) — catches "cheap but growth is negative
  too" and "expensive but justified by growth," which P/E alone can't
- FCF yield (FCF / market cap) — harder to game with accounting than
  P/E
- Interest coverage (EBIT / interest expense) — solvency check
- Margin stability (variance of operating margin over 5 years, not just
  current-period) — rough proxy for moat
- Sector-relative P/E (percentile vs. sector median, not a flat 20) —
  fixes flat P/E punishing software and rewarding banks for no reason
- Insider ownership / insider buying — behavioral signal, qualitative
- Check 1st analyst target estimate
- Operating cash flow vs. total debt — good coverage ratio
- Check earnings date

## Dividends
- Payout ratio < 70%
- History: increased over the years, or at least constant every year

## REITs
- Price/Book < 1
